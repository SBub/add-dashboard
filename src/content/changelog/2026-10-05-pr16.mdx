Second production release. Everything merged into develop since the first release, five of them built end to end by ADW from a labelled issue.

## Features

- Format dates on the dashboard in European format ([#10](https://github.com/SBub/adw-dashboard/issues/10), [PR #13](https://github.com/SBub/adw-dashboard/pull/13))
- Paginate a project's completed history with a keyset bookmark, three per page ([#17](https://github.com/SBub/adw-dashboard/issues/17), [PR #18](https://github.com/SBub/adw-dashboard/pull/18))
