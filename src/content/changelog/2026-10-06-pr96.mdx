The Summary is now the home page, and the projects moved to /projects. It shows totals instead of medians, counts cached tokens on their own, and puts a Total row under each day's table. Queue rows are down to one line.

## Features

- The Summary shows sums instead of medians, with a column for cached tokens read ([#80](https://github.com/SBub/adw-dashboard/issues/80), [PR #81](https://github.com/SBub/adw-dashboard/pull/81))

## Fixes

- The Summary's Project and Window filters look the same in Safari as in Chrome and line up with the Apply button ([#82](https://github.com/SBub/adw-dashboard/issues/82), [PR #84](https://github.com/SBub/adw-dashboard/pull/84))
- The section info tooltip now shows above the Queue's "next" marker instead of under it ([#88](https://github.com/SBub/adw-dashboard/issues/88), [PR #93](https://github.com/SBub/adw-dashboard/pull/93))

## Chores

- Took the summary charts (#66, PR #75) back out of develop ([PR #78](https://github.com/SBub/adw-dashboard/pull/78))
- The Summary became the home page at /, and the project list moved to /projects ([#83](https://github.com/SBub/adw-dashboard/issues/83), [PR #87](https://github.com/SBub/adw-dashboard/pull/87))
- Each day's totals moved from a strip above the table to a Total row at the bottom, lined up with its columns ([#85](https://github.com/SBub/adw-dashboard/issues/85), [PR #90](https://github.com/SBub/adw-dashboard/pull/90))
- Queue rows lost the label chip and the wait line: one line per row ([#86](https://github.com/SBub/adw-dashboard/issues/86), [PR #92](https://github.com/SBub/adw-dashboard/pull/92))
- The home page opens with a short intro on what ADW is and what this dashboard shows, instead of the heading "Summary" ([#89](https://github.com/SBub/adw-dashboard/issues/89), [PR #94](https://github.com/SBub/adw-dashboard/pull/94))
